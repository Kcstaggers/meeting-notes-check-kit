The rule for this exercise

Keep explicit assignments or commitments that remain open at the end of the meeting. Leave out suggestions, canceled or completed work, and work that depends on an unmet condition. An explicit assignment counts unless it is disputed or withdrawn. Leave unknown owners and unresolved dates blank.

This is a chosen exercise rule. Teams may track proposals and conditional work separately. Short fictional cases do not measure general model accuracy.

    Reveal the reference and paired change

    Use the disagreement.

    A wrong owner, a fabricated deadline and a missed task are different problems. The report keeps them separate. In extraction mode, a person must confirm task matching; an unfamiliar task stays unresolved until reviewed.

    Try a case, then open its pair. Look for the one line that changes the answer. For prompt comparisons, keep the answers hidden and record the model, date and exact prompt. This kit includes no measured model results.
